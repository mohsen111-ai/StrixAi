STRIX

Motion

Micro-interactions

Everything is 110 to 180 ms with a fast-out curve, and only moves transform and opacity, so nothing lags. In the terminal the same ideas run as 60 ms frame updates: spinner frames, a blinking block caret, a pulsing bar beside the active step. Hover or tap each one.

Display
Chakra Petch
Angular, technical, a little military. Headings, the wordmark, buttons. Wide letter-spacing, uppercase.
JetBrains Mono · every terminal pane, code, numbers, keybinds
Rules
  • Crimson marks only what is active, wrong, or the brand. Never decoration.
  • Success is plain white with a check. Failure is crimson with a cross.
  • Corners are cut at 45 degrees, never rounded.
  • Lines are 1 px, white at 10 to 22 percent. Glow is reserved for the active element.
  • Works in 24-bit color, and degrades to the 16-color terminal palette.
Emblem

The owl

A shield head with two ear tufts, hexagonal eyes, a diamond beak and a chevron chest. Built only from straight lines, so it also draws in ASCII.
